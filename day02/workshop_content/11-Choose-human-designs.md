# 11 · Choose the three human designs

**Goal:** give every participant a design decision and lock a prospective human set.

1. Each participant picks or draws one candidate and explains its structural rationale.
2. A group of three selects one candidate per participant. A group of two makes a third candidate together, testing a distinct hypothesis where possible.
3. Compare the three drawings, remove accidental duplicates and check their SMILES again.
4. Record the change relative to LSD, expected 5-HT2A affinity direction, expected selectivity direction and main uncertainty for each.
5. Save the three structures and predictions before opening the AI design conversation. Nominate your predicted human winner in the worksheet.
