# 12 · Submit the six designs

**Goal:** six molecules submitted, with IDs you can trace through the rest of the day.

1. Open the **[submission app](https://cpdse-edux.github.io/UCPH-PhD-AI-data-driven-drug-design/day02/apps/smiles/)**.
2. Submit one molecule at a time. The app validates the SMILES, draws the structure and generates a compound ID.
3. Check the drawn structure before you submit. This is your last chance to catch a mistake cheaply.
4. Note each generated **compound ID** against the hypothesis it came from.

Compound IDs look like `G4HPeter1` or `G4AIClaude1` — group, origin, designer, number. Your docking results come back carrying these names, and so does the selectivity calculator at the end, so the ID is what ties a score back to a molecule and a hypothesis.

5. Reload the **Dashboard** and confirm your group has three Human and three AI entries.

> **The pooled library is the instructors' job.** We collect every group's submissions and hand back one `.smi` file. You do not need to download or assemble anything.
> {: .tip }

> **Dock your own group's compounds, not everyone's.** The pooled file contains every group's molecules. Docking all of them will not finish in the time available. Step 13 shows you how to cut it down to yours plus the LSD control.
> {: .warn }

**Checkpoint**

- [ ] Six submissions, three Human and three AI.
- [ ] You can match every compound ID back to its molecule and hypothesis.
- [ ] The Dashboard shows all six under your group.
- [ ] Your predicted winners are nominated before any score exists.
