# 10 · Choose the three human designs

**Goal:** three molecules, chosen and locked, before any AI is involved.

1. Each participant presents one candidate and the structural reason for it.
2. A group of three takes one design per person. A group of two picks two, then builds a third together — ideally testing a different hypothesis from the first two.
3. Compare the three. Drop accidental duplicates and check the SMILES once more.
4. Nominate the one you think will win.

**Human** here means the chemical idea came from a person. If the AI suggested the modification and you redrew it, it is still an AI design. Asking a chatbot which button to press in Maestro does not make a design AI-origin.

> **Discuss** — your three designs: are they three genuinely different hypotheses, or the same idea three times with different substituents? If they all fail, how much will you have learned?
> {: .discuss }

> **Lock these in now.** Save the structures and your predictions before you open a chatbot. If you let AI suggestions influence your human set, there is nothing left to compare at the end of the day.
> {: .warn }

**Checkpoint**

- [ ] Exactly three human designs, saved.
- [ ] Each one has a written prediction attached.
- [ ] You have nominated a predicted winner.
- [ ] No chatbot has seen your design problem yet.
