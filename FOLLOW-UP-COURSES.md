# Suggested follow-up courses

[Course overview](README.md) · [Course program](PROGRAM.md)

Want to go further after this week? These courses build on the topics we covered, from databases to data visualization.

## Databases

- [Training at the European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training/)

## Computational drug design

- [Schrödinger online certification courses](https://www.schrodinger.com/online-certification-courses/): asynchronous, self-paced online courses.
- [TeachOpenCADD](https://www.drugdesign.org/tutorials/teachopencadd/): free online courses.

## Protein modeling and peptide binder design

- [Advanced Methods in Protein Design](https://phdcourses.ku.dk/DetailKursus.aspx?id=114557): offered in February at the Center for Protein Design, UCPH. The next run is 1–12 February 2027; sign up by 3 January 2027.

## Data visualization

- [Data Visualization](https://www.sdu.dk/en/om-sdu/institutter-centre/imada_matematik_og_datalogi/evu-kurser/courses/data-visualization): a 4-hour workshop at SDU.
- [Data visualization for life science](https://media.medfarm.uu.se/kurser/course_view.php?id=1421&lang=en): a one-week online course at Uppsala University that runs every year. Apply now: the deadline for the 2026 course is 15 November.
- [Data visualisation workshop](https://ddeacademy.dk/events/workshop/data-visualisation-workshop-from-theoretical-foundations-to-implementation-and-communication/): a two-day course in Aarhus. The latest one took place on 5–6 November 2026, but it is likely to run again.

## Organizations that run courses in Copenhagen and Denmark

- [Center for Pharmaceutical Data Science Education (CPDSE)](https://cpdse.dk/learning-resources/)
- [Center for Health Data Science (HeaDS), SUND, UCPH](https://heads.ku.dk/course)
- [Danish Data Science Academy (DDSA)](https://ddsa.dk/)
