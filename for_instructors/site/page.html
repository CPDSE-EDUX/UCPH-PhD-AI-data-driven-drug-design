University of Copenhagen · 9–13 November 2026

AI & data-driven drug design

A one-week PhD course on the tools, data and evidence behind molecular discovery.

Mornings are lectures with examples from industry. Afternoons are hands-on workshops where you search databases, design and dock molecules, and test what computational predictions can and cannot tell you.

What you will practise

  • Find and interpret drug, target and disease data.
  • Turn molecular interactions into ligand design hypotheses.
  • Explore AI/ML tools for protein and peptide design.
  • Choose visual representations and analyse molecular data.
  • Critically assess AI suggestions and computational predictions.
